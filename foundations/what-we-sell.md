# What We Sell

We sell **Aanish's engineering capability** — a senior full-stack engineer and
technical lead who takes products from inception to revenue, accelerated by AI
but governed by real architecture experience. If you can explain *that* simply,
you can sell it.

## The 60-second pitch

> Aanish is a senior software engineer with 7+ years hands-on and a background
> leading enterprise projects. He builds the whole product — backend, frontend,
> infrastructure — and has taken products from a blank page to generating
> revenue. Over the last few years he's folded AI (Claude and others) into how
> he works, so delivery is dramatically faster. The difference from the typical
> "AI builder": because he's architected real enterprise systems, he avoids the
> structural mistakes AI makes when it's left to code on its own. You get
> startup speed with senior-engineer judgment.

## The problem we solve

Teams are shipping faster with AI — and quietly accumulating a mess:
architectural dead-ends, brittle code, security and scaling problems that only
show up once there are real users. Founders and small teams either can't build
the product at all, or they've "vibe-coded" a prototype that can't become a real
business. What they need is a senior engineer who can **move at AI speed and keep
the architecture sound.**

# Discovery Calls

Discovery is where deals are won or lost. The goal is not to pitch — it's to
understand the prospect's world well enough to know if we can genuinely help, and
to earn a real next step.

## Before the call

* Research the account and person (5–10 min): recent news, role, likely pain.
* Set **one** primary objective and the next step you want to earn.
* Have your [MEDDICC](../process/qualification.md) checklist open.

## The structure (25–30 min)

1. **Open (2 min)** — set the agenda, confirm time, get permission to ask questions.
2. **Discover (15–20 min)** — their situation, pain, impact, and what they've tried.
3. **Align (5 min)** — recap what you heard; confirm whether it's a fit (both ways).
4. **Next step (3 min)** — agree a specific, dated next action before you hang up.

## Questions that work

> **Fill in:** Add our best discovery questions. Starters below.

**Situation**
* Walk me through how you handle {{area}} today.
* What tools / process are involved?

**Pain & impact**
* Where does that break down?
* What does that cost you — time, money, risk?
* What happens if nothing changes in the next 6–12 months?

**Decision**
* Who else cares about solving this?
* How would a decision like this get made on your side?

## The rules

* **Talk less than half the time.** If you're pitching, you're not discovering.
* **Quantify the pain.** "It's annoying" isn't a business case; "it costs us X"
  is.
* **Never leave without a mutual next step** on the calendar.
* **Log it the same day** in the CRM, against the MEDDICC fields.

## After the call

* Send a recap email: what you heard, agreed next step, and date.
* Update the deal stage and MEDDICC fields honestly.

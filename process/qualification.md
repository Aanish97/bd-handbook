A gap in any one of these is the reason deals slip.

## How to use it

* MEDDICC is a **checklist, not a script.** Weave the questions into
  [discovery](../playbooks/discovery-calls.md) naturally.
* A **champion is tested, not assumed.** Give them a small ask (an intro, a data
  point). If they deliver, they're real.
* If you can't name the **economic buyer** by the Qualified stage, you don't have
  a qualified deal.

## Red flags

* No metric → no business case → no urgency.
* "Everyone loves it" but no single champion → stalls in procurement.
* Can't describe the decision process → you don't control the deal; they do.

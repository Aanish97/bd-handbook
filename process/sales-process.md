## The rules

* **No skipping stages.** A deal at "Proposal" without confirmed pain is a deal
  you'll lose late and loud.
* **Every stage has a mutual next step** on the calendar before you hang up.
* **Update the stage the same day** it changes. Pipeline reviews run off the CRM,
  not your memory.
* **A deal with no activity in _N_ days is at risk** — flag it or close it.

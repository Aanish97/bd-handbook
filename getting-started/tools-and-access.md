## Ground rules

* **Everything goes in the CRM.** If it isn't logged, it didn't happen. See
  [CRM Conventions](../process/crm-conventions.md).
* **One source of truth per thing.** Don't keep a personal spreadsheet of deals.
* **Security:** never share credentials or export customer data to personal
  accounts. Report anything that looks like a phishing attempt.

# Glossary

Shared language so everyone means the same thing. Add GrowthCode-specific terms
and acronyms as they come up.

## Sales terms

| Term | Meaning |
| ---- | ------- |
| **ICP** | Ideal Customer Profile — the company type we target. |
| **Persona** | A specific role we sell to within an account. |
| **MEDDICC** | Our qualification framework — see [Qualification](../process/qualification.md). |
| **Champion** | An internal advocate who sells for us when we're not in the room. |
| **Economic buyer** | The person who controls the budget and final yes. |
| **Trigger event** | A change at an account that creates a reason to reach out now. |
| **SQL / SAL** | Sales-qualified / sales-accepted lead. |
| **ARR / TCV** | Annual recurring revenue / total contract value. |
| **Pipeline** | The sum of open deals, by stage. |
| **Close rate** | Won deals ÷ total qualified deals. |
| **Cost of inaction** | What the prospect loses by not solving the problem. |

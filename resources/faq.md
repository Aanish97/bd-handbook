## Selling Aanish's services

**What exactly do we sell?**
Senior full-stack engineering and technical leadership — see
[What We Sell](../foundations/what-we-sell.md).

**What's the 60-second pitch?**
"AI speed, senior-engineer judgment" — the full version is in
[What We Sell](../foundations/what-we-sell.md) and
[Positioning](../foundations/positioning-and-messaging.md).

**Who's the ideal client?**
Early-stage founders and small teams who need a product built or a stalled /
AI-built prototype turned into a real product — see
[ICP & Personas](../foundations/icp-and-personas.md).

**What makes Aanish different from an AI builder or a cheap dev shop?**
He moves at AI speed *and* has 7+ years of hands-on enterprise architecture
experience, so he avoids the structural mistakes AI makes unsupervised. One
senior person covers backend, frontend, and infra. See
[Positioning](../foundations/positioning-and-messaging.md).

**What's the tech stack?**
Django / DRF, React, Next.js, plus DevOps / server-side setup. For anything
outside that, check fit before pitching.

**A prospect says we're too expensive — what now?**
Reframe to value and the cost of a rebuild — see
[Objection Handling](../playbooks/objection-handling.md).
